# Code of Conduct

We want PullLamp to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or
identity.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these standards, and
may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the item, or contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
